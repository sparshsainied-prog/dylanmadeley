About me

"I get an idea and can't resist the compulsion to immediately follow through."

Born in Toronto, living in Mimico by the shores of Lake Ontario. I wrote The Gift-Knight's Quest in 2008 and released it in 2015, then took it and its sequels down and rewrote the whole thing, starting from Prince Ewald the Brave. From 2012 to 2022 I was a copy editor for Auxiliary Magazine. You'll also find me at Ad Astra.
